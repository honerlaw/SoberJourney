# Replan log: sponsor-chat-backend

## 2026-10-06 — Add crisis-resource guidance to the sponsor system prompt (owner decision)

**Original plan**: `systemPrompt.mts` crisis-hotline guidance stayed byte-for-byte unchanged ("Do not recommend hotlines or crisis resources unless the user explicitly indicates they are in crisis or asks for them") as an open owner product decision; the safety-block fallback avoided hotline language; success criterion "crisis-hotline guidance is byte-for-byte unchanged".

**What changed**: The owner decided, verbatim: "crisis hotline - yea, but don't be to straight forward / easy to trigger it, e.g. talking about relapsing is somewhat common in recover (e.g. I really want to drink or do a drug or something right now) is kind of common and we dont need to send to a crisis hotline for that." Requirements: offer crisis resources (988, local emergency services, SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357) on genuine signs of acute danger (suicidal thoughts, self-harm, intent to harm others, possible overdose / medical emergency, immediate danger), stated or clearly implied; never for ordinary cravings, urges, "I really want to drink/use right now", having relapsed, or general distress; warm, brief, keep supporting; US numbers marked US; short; reconsider the safety fallback in the same spirit. The owner also approved persist-before-generate. PR #41 was still open and unmerged (the proposal's "Shipped" status was written by promote before merge), so neither the old prompt change nor the earlier fallback constant ever reached the epic base or production.

**New plan** (as implemented in `packages/server/src/network/rpc/route/conversation/sponsorChat/utils/systemPrompt.mts` and `fallback.mts`):
- Guideline line 6 becomes: "Only bring up hotlines or crisis resources in the situations described under "Crisis resources" below, or when the user asks for them."
- A new "Crisis resources" section: cravings/urges/wanting to use now/relapse/general distress are normal recovery, not a crisis — no hotline, sponsor support instead (coping step, reach out to someone they trust, reminders of progress); resources only on genuine acute-danger signs, stated or clearly implied (suicidal thoughts or not wanting to be alive, self-harm, intent to harm others, possible overdose, severe withdrawal — seizures, hallucinations, confusion — or another medical emergency, immediate danger); **acute danger takes priority** over the craving/relapse carve-out when both appear; warm, brief, keep supporting; US mapping: 988 for suicide/self-harm, 911 for overdose, severe withdrawal, violence or immediate danger; outside the US the local emergency number or crisis line; SAMHSA (US) mentioned when substance use is part of it or they ask about treatment (a deliberate refinement: SAMHSA is a treatment line, not an emergency line); the older distraction / "this conversation counts as reaching out" guidance is scoped to ordinary cravings, not acute danger.
- `SAFETY_FALLBACK_REPLY`: shorter supportive opener plus a conditional line: "If you're thinking about hurting yourself or you're in danger, please reach out now: in the US, call or text 988 or call 911; elsewhere, your local emergency number. I'm still here with you."
- Proposal edits: Approach F crisis line, the Success criterion that required the prompt unchanged, Open Questions (crisis decision and "Owner review" of persist-before-generate both closed). Knowledge edits (entries added by this unit, not yet merged): `2026-10-06-decision-sponsor-chat-persist-before-generate` (owner approved; fallback wording), `2026-10-06-reference-sponsor-chat-backend-surface` (crisis guidance line); new `2026-10-06-decision-sponsor-chat-crisis-guidance` quoting the owner.
- Tests: `utils/__tests__/systemPrompt.test.mts` (carve-out, triggers incl. severe withdrawal, precedence, US labels and mapping, SAMHSA, keep supporting, conditional fallback); `runSponsorChat` sends the crisis section. Prompt behaviour itself cannot be unit-tested; the PR body carries a manual smoke list.
- Accepted trade-offs: the fallback is shown on every safety block (the prompt is gated, the fallback is conditional text because the reason for the block is unknown); a blocked turn is excluded from later history, so a blocked crisis disclosure is not visible to the model on later turns (the fallback invites the user to say more); the prompt change applies to all existing conversations immediately, with no API change.

**Post-acceptance review fixes (2026-10-06, triage, not a new plan)**: independent code review of the crisis commit led to: harmful-content blocks only (`SAFETY`, `PROHIBITED_CONTENT`) get the crisis-line fallback, other blocks get a neutral `BLOCKED_FALLBACK_REPLY`, and `buildHistory` excludes both; the carve-out adds "neither is a high urge or low mood on a check-in by itself"; the overdose sign adds "including using again after time sober, when tolerance is lower, with signs like heavy drowsiness, slow breathing, or being hard to wake"; prompt tests assert key tokens instead of whole sentences.
